# Institution Payment accounts

This section contains wallet information for your institution. Access it at <a href={process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_PARTNERS_URL + '/wallet'} className="underline" target='_blank'>Payment accounts management</a>.

There are two account types:

- **Top-up account:** Default account type for newly onboarded institutions. You must top up in advance before using services. Keep your account balance positive to avoid service interruption. When balance drops below the threshold, Owner receives an email reminder to top up. Each order deducts the corresponding amount from this account.

- **Debit account:** Available for eligible institutions. Contact SimplifyTrip for eligibility conditions. With a debit account, transactions can be made before top-up and charges are accumulated as debt. At month-end, SimplifyTrip issues a statement and sends debt notification to Owner. You have 5 days to settle debt; overdue debt may lock the account and interrupt services.

<Callout type="info">
 	You can choose either account type as your default payment account. SimplifyTrip deducts transaction amounts from the default account.
</Callout>

### Payment accounts in Test Mode

SimplifyTrip provides separate environments to support safe testing.

In Test Mode, all transactions are simulated, so Payment accounts in this mode are virtual. Funds are for testing only; you can top up freely without real cost. Debit account debt in Test Mode does not require real settlement.
